Every autumn we review the colony census and find the cats that aren't neutered yet, especially newcomers.

## What you'll see over the coming weeks

We'll hold trapping days with our partner vet clinics. If you live near a colony, you may see **traps covered with a blanket**. They're watched at all times: please don't touch them.

## How you can help

- Driving cats to the clinic and back to their colony.
- Fostering a cat while it recovers from surgery (one to three days).
- Donating towards the cost of neutering.

Want to help? Message us on WhatsApp.
