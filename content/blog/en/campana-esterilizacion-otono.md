Every autumn we review the census of every colony and find the cats that are not neutered yet, especially newcomers.

## What you will see over the coming weeks

We will hold trapping days coordinated with our partner vet clinics. If you live near a colony, you may see **traps covered with a blanket**: they are watched at all times.

## How you can help

- Driving cats to the clinic and back to their colony.
- Fostering a cat while it recovers from surgery (one or two nights).
- Making a donation towards the cost of neutering.

If you would like to lend a hand, message us on WhatsApp.
