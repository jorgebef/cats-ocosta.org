Until now we organised almost everything over WhatsApp. With this website we want to explain what we do more clearly and make it easier to get involved.

## What you'll find

- What the **TNR method** is and what Law 7/2023 says.
- How to **volunteer**, however much time you have.
- This **blog**, with grants, field work, campaigns and fundraising.

The site uses no cookies or analytics: we just want you to read it.
