Until now we organised almost everything over WhatsApp. With this website we want to explain what we do better and make it easier to join in.

## What you will find

- An explanation of the **TNR method** and what Law 7/2023 says.
- How to **volunteer**, depending on how much time you have.
- This **blog**, with grants, field work, campaigns and fundraising.

The site uses no cookies or analytics: we just want you to read it.
