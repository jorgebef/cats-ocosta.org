On trapping day we hold back food beforehand, so the cats go into the traps hungry and it all goes faster.

## Trapping

We set the traps at the usual feeding time and watch them from a distance. As soon as a cat goes in, we cover the trap with a blanket to calm it down.

## At the clinic

The cats go straight to the clinic, where the vet team:

- neuters and deworms them,
- checks their general health,
- and clips the tip of one ear so they can be identified.

## Back home

They recover for one to three days, as the vet advises, and then go back to their colony, **to the exact spot** where we trapped them.
