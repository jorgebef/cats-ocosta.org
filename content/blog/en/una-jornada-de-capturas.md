The day before, no food is left at the colony, so cats come to the traps hungry and trapping is quicker.

## Trapping

We set the traps at the usual feeding time and watch them from a distance. As soon as a cat goes in, the trap is covered with a blanket to calm it down.

## At the clinic

The cats go straight to the clinic, where the vet team:

- neuters and deworms them,
- checks their general health,
- and clips the tip of one ear so they can be identified.

## Back home

After neutering they recover overnight and return to their colony, **to the very same spot**, once the vet says so.
